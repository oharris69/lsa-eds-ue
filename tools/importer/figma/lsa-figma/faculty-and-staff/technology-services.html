Technology Services

Your academic major doesn’t determine your career—you do. The Hub’s Industry Groups can nurture your curiosity and fuel the journey you take to discover career possibilities. At the heart of it, these industry-specific groups help create connections between your education, your goals, and your network. Connect with students who share similar fields of interests, get specialized industry resources, and access in-the-field opportunities that’ll help you understand the realities of these working-world industries.

What do you gain from Industry Groups?

  • Gain access to industry-specific and specialized information, resources, networking events, and career opportunities
  • Reflect on your personal mission and vision as you an embark on a professionally supported career exploration journey
  • Build and grow a peer network with future professionals in your industry while giving and receiving peer mentoring
  • Advance your professional pursuits and help form your professional identity
  • Questions? Reach out to recruit-lsaopphub@umich.edu about the Hub’s industry groups.
